DC BEGINS

DC Comics was originally founded in 1934 as National Allied Publications. The publisher ushered in the superhero era with Action Comics #1 in 1938, introducing Superman to the world, followed by Batman in Detective Comics #27 in 1939. The initials "DC" come from the flagship Detective Comics line, which officially became the company's full name in 1977.

FAMOUS DC TEAMS

JUSTICE LEAGUE

The Justice League is DC's premier superhero team. Made up of iconic heroes like Superman, Batman, Wonder Woman, and The Flash, they defend Earth from cosmic and apocalyptic threats.

TEEN TITANS

A team composed of young heroes and sidekicks led by Robin. They protect Jump City and battle powerful adversaries while navigating the struggles of growing up.

SUICIDE SQUAD

Officially known as Task Force X, this secret government unit deploys incarcerated supervillains on high-risk covert operations in exchange for commuted prison sentences.
