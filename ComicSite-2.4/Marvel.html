MARVEL BEGINS

Marvel Comics (at the time known as Timely Comics) was founded in the year 1939, releasing their first comic in August of that year. Their first comic features the first apparences of both The Android Human Torch and Namor the Sub-Mariner. Marvel become the comic company's name due to a bussiness stratagy where Timely Comics used various shell companies to relase their comics. One of these companies was called Marvel Comics, and it's name was first used as the primary name of the company in 1961. The modern era of Marvel also started in 1961, when The Fantastic Four #1 comic was released.
